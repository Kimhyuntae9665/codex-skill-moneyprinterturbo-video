# Prompt Templates

Use these prompts directly or adapt them.

## S&P 500 Quality/Value Screen

```text
Use $us-stock-research to screen the current S&P 500 for high-quality value stocks. Rank by ROE, ROIC, FCF yield, P/E, PEG, revenue growth, debt/equity, dividend yield, and 1Y momentum. Use public sources first, cite every source, show missing-data names separately, and give me the top 30 with a Korean explanation.
```

## Single Stock Deep Dive

```text
Use $us-stock-research to analyze [TICKER] in detail. Pull SEC fundamentals, historical prices, dividends, recent news and filings, valuation ratios, ROE/ROIC/PEG, peer comparison, and bull/base/bear scenarios. Separate reported data from calculated metrics and show data limitations.
```

## Dividend Stock Screen

```text
Use $us-stock-research to find US dividend stocks with sustainable yield. Screen for dividend yield, 5Y dividend growth, payout ratio, FCF payout, ROE, leverage, earnings stability, and recent dividend/news risks. Compare yield to US Treasury yields and explain in Korean.
```

## Historical Price Check

```text
Use $us-stock-research to find [TICKER]'s historical price on [DATE]. Show raw close, adjusted close, nearest trading day if the market was closed, split/dividend handling, total return to today, and the data source.
```

## News and Filing Update

```text
Use $us-stock-research to summarize the latest material news and SEC filings for [TICKER]. Separate confirmed filings/company statements from media reports, estimate fundamental impact, and list what to verify next.
```
