# Story and Visuals

Build either one focused Short around a hidden economic mechanism or a connected dossier of two to four mechanisms that jointly support one company-specific thesis.

## Candidate score

Score each candidate from 0 to 2 on:

- surprise to a financially literate viewer;
- A-grade evidence;
- direct economic consequence;
- company specificity;
- visual demonstrability;
- timely relevance;
- strength after the bear case is shown.

For focused mode, select the highest total only if evidence and economic consequence both score 2. For connected-dossier mode, select two to four finalists that cover different mechanisms, share one defensible umbrella thesis, and each contain an A-grade anchor. A colorful rumor with weak proof can add a clearly labeled social-media beat, but it does not replace the anchor or control the verdict.

Do not average weak candidates into a strong-looking package. Score and verify each dossier item separately. Keep overflow candidates in the research audit as seeds for later Shorts.

## Story archetypes

### 1. Paid twice

The company earns once from a product sale and again from usage, financing, service, or revenue participation.

Visual: product moves right; cash returns once; a second usage loop lights up and returns a different colored cash stream. Name the contractual floor and the unknown share separately.

### 2. Capacity backstop

A company helps a partner finance infrastructure by guaranteeing part of the demand or residual capacity.

Visual: a utilization gauge, customer demand filling the first portion, a clearly labeled backstop filling only the contractually supported remainder, then upside sharing above a floor. Do not shade 100% unless the contract covers 100%.

### 3. Supply fortress

The company spends or commits capital across chips, memory, packaging, networking, power, land, or shells to reduce bottlenecks.

Visual: a physical supply route with dated capacity commitments at the correct stage. Show the tradeoff as a second lane: access advantage versus cancellation, inventory, or concentration risk.

### 4. Full-stack capture

The apparent product is only one layer of a broader system.

Visual: reveal the layers one at a time—compute, networking, systems, software, cloud or financing—then highlight which layers have disclosed revenue and which are strategic inference. Avoid allocating combined-segment revenue to a single layer.

### 5. Regulatory moat crack

A rule, standard, or access change weakens the bottleneck that protected a business.

Visual: regulator opens a previously closed gate; customers gain a second route; price or volume sensitivity changes. Use the final regulator text, not only an X summary.

### 6. Capital-allocation fingerprint

The timing and result of buybacks, investments, debt, or acquisitions reveal management behavior.

Visual: share-price band plus quarterly repurchase dots, actual diluted share-count result, and strategic-investment lane. Authorization alone is not execution.

### 7. Indirect operating signal

A product price, lead time, used-equipment price, app rank, hiring pattern, or facility utilization may reveal demand before a headline metric.

Visual: clue first, official KPI second, alternative explanation third. The clue cannot carry the verdict alone.

## Hook patterns

Write three versions and choose the one that can be understood within three seconds.

- `이 서버가 비면, 누가 돈을 낼까요? 계약서에는 [회사]가 나옵니다.`
- `[회사]는 제품을 팔고 끝이 아닙니다. 고객 매출에서도 다시 돈을 받습니다.`
- `[회사]가 묶어 둔 건 칩이 아닙니다. 전기와 공장 자리까지입니다.`
- `뉴스는 [제품]만 보는데, 돈의 흐름은 [숨은 레이어]에서 한 번 더 생깁니다.`
- `X에서는 [강한 주장]이라고 합니다. 공시가 확인해 주는 건 어디까지일까요?`

Use a hook only when the claim ledger supports each premise. Mystery comes from an unanswered causal question, not from hiding the ticker.

## Mechanism map

Every diagram element must include:

```text
object_id | label | role | source_claim_id | entrance | exit | safe_box
arrow_id  | from | to | thing_moving | direction | source_claim_id
sequence  | persistent_subject | states | camera_path | transition_purpose
```

Use different visual tokens for:

- hardware or capacity;
- cash payment;
- equity ownership;
- contractual guarantee;
- customer demand;
- analysis or optional upside.

Never place arrows behind hero numbers or through text. Test the first frame, peak motion, landing frame, and boundary frame at phone size.

## Continuous mechanism animation

Use the motion logic extracted in [channel-motion-study.md](channel-motion-study.md) when the story is about an invisible contract, flow, or system. Build a miniature world around one persistent subject instead of replacing the whole screen with a new card every sentence.

The preferred reveal sequence is:

1. **Impossible or surprising state:** show the subject already in motion or under stress while the hook asks what appears impossible.
2. **Isolation:** remove background clutter, center the same subject, and attach one short label.
3. **Hidden layer:** cut away, explode, peel, or make the exterior transparent so the concealed mechanism becomes visible.
4. **Causal motion:** animate one force, capacity, ownership, or cash flow at a time along a clear path.
5. **Counterfactual or comparison:** briefly show the common mental model beside the actual structure, then return to the actual structure for the payoff.

Every state must teach a new causal step. Decorative camera motion without a new fact does not count as a state change.

Choose the camera for meaning:

- wide or aerial view establishes scale and location;
- tracking preserves continuity while an object moves through the system;
- top-down view clarifies routes and allocation;
- orbit reveals topology or a relationship hidden from the first angle;
- cutaway, x-ray, or transparent shell reveals an internal contract or stack;
- macro close-up makes one component, number, or document phrase the hero;
- split screen compares old versus new, expected versus actual, or bull versus bear using aligned scale.

Default to a meaningful visual change every roughly 0.8–2.5 seconds. An object state, material state, topology, force, flow, environmental response, or camera move into new information may provide the change, but it must land on a narration clause. Keep spatial continuity across cuts so the viewer knows they are still looking at the same system.

## Translate the grammar to company stories

Physicalize intangible finance without changing its meaning:

| Business concept | Motion treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Capacity backstop | Dark unused racks become visible; customer demand fills first; only the documented backstop portion lights next |
| Minimum-revenue floor | A transparent floor rises to the disclosed threshold; lender funding appears only after the floor locks |
| Revenue share | Hardware cash returns once, then a separate usage loop sends an unlabeled-size share back above the floor |
| Supply commitment | Foundry, memory, packaging, networking, power, and shell appear as reserved lanes; committed capacity is not drawn as current revenue |
| Full stack | The same AI factory peels into compute, networking, systems, software, and distribution layers |
| Equity alignment | A proportional ownership slice attaches to the partner; keep investment cash separate from operating revenue |
| Regulatory moat change | A formerly closed gate opens a second route; customer flow reallocates only as supported by the rule |
| Buyback quality | Repurchase dots enter treasury while issued shares enter from another side; the net share-count gauge resolves the result |

Use a large misconception mark or freeze-frame reset at most once per Short. It should remove the wrong mental model, not become a recurring decorative effect.

Keep the environment restrained: neutral materials or a real photographic base, one company-relevant accent color, and a brighter highlight only for the active causal object. Do not imitate the reference channel's exact palette, labels, recurring marks, or watermarks.

## Real-image grammar

Use real imagery to establish the physical world of the mechanism:

- data centers, substations, server racks, products, retail shelves, hospitals, aircraft, stores, factories, ports, or executives;
- company and partner logos only with source and trademark context recorded;
- contract or filing excerpts as cropped proof, not as unreadable full-page screenshots.

Add slow reframing or parallax to still photos and change the meaningful visual state every few seconds. Avoid a single background photo under a minute of animated text.

When possible, transition from a real establishing image into a matched 2.5D or 3D model of the same facility, product, store, hospital, or data center. Preserve angle, silhouette, and dominant color so the change feels like entering the object rather than cutting to a presentation slide.

## Screen hierarchy

Each beat gets one dominant object and one spoken idea. Use the production skill's safe zones and typography rules. A strong evidence beat normally contains:

1. a short conversational headline;
2. one hero number or contract phrase;
3. one visual mechanism;
4. one short source strip.

Keep Korean captions to one short spoken clause. Object labels belong near the object and must not compete with captions. Reveal measurements, percentages, and dates through anchored brackets, gauges, or tracked callouts rather than floating text with no physical referent.

## Closing bridge

For company-story mode, end by resolving the opening visual and changing the viewer's mental model:

```text
familiar object -> impossible result -> wrong explanation -> hidden mechanism -> visible consequence -> company redefined
```

The final line should answer the first question or give the first frame a new meaning. It may explain what the company really controls, why a strange contract exists, how a hidden system works, or which risk is transferred. It does not need a stock recommendation.

For investment-story mode, add the valuation chain after the mechanism:

```text
mechanism -> measurable KPI -> earnings or cash-flow consequence -> valuation assumption -> verdict
```

Do not force a valuation or recommendation into company-story mode merely because market data is available.
