# Wall Street Edge Shorts

Find many candidate stories on X and Reddit, prove the factual spine of the best candidates with primary documents, and turn one focused reveal or a coherent bundle of two to four hidden business mechanisms into a finished Korean Short. Direct the picture as a continuous animated world: establish a physical subject, enter or cut through it, animate the hidden process, and end on a visible consequence. Do not reduce the story to stock photos, cards, charts, or kinetic captions.

This skill owns research, story direction, renderer selection, and the motion contract. `moneyprinterturbo-video` may provide Korean TTS, subtitle timing, audio mixing, final assembly, thumbnail export, and technical QA, but it does not dictate the picture grammar for company-story mode. When the user asks for a video, finish the research packet and the motion master; do not stop at a list of posts, script, or storyboard.

## Choose the editorial mode

- **Company story — default:** use when the user asks for an interesting, hidden, strange, under-covered, or rumor-like story about a company. Resolve a curiosity question through animated cause and effect. Ten-year P/E, current price, target price, and `추천/관망/비추천` are optional and normally omitted.
- **Investment story:** use only when the user asks for stock analysis, valuation, entry price, recommendation, shareholder return, or whether the stock is attractive. Add the dated price, valuation bridge, risk, invalidation condition, and explicit verdict required by `moneyprinterturbo-video`.

Do not turn company-story mode into a compressed equity research report. Its ending should deliver a memorable answer or redefine what the company controls. Do not turn investment-story mode into rumor-only entertainment; its verdict still rests on documented facts and reproducible calculations.

## Editorial promise

The viewer should leave knowing at least one surprising mechanism that ordinary headline coverage misses. A focused Short may carry one reveal; a roundup or identity-redefinition Short may combine two to four related reveals under one thesis. Every included story has all four properties:

1. **Specific:** replacing the ticker with another company makes the thesis false.
2. **Causal:** it shows how a contract, asset, distribution channel, capacity commitment, or capital-allocation choice changes revenue, margin, risk, or strategic control.
3. **Provable:** at least one decisive premise is supported by a current primary source.
4. **Visual:** the mechanism can be shown as money, capacity, ownership, product, or contract flows rather than a list of ratios.

Do not make a generic news recap or an unexplained list of X opinions. Rumor-like Wall Street chatter may appear when it is the story's attraction, but the screen and narration must identify it as chatter and immediately show what public documents confirm, leave open, or contradict.

## Workflow

### 1. Define the hunt

Record the issuer, ticker, as-of date, viewer question, why the subject matters now, and three query families:

- hidden mechanism: `contract`, `take-or-pay`, `capacity`, `minimum guarantee`, `revenue share`, `full stack`, `distribution`;
- operating clue: product price, lead time, utilization, power, backlog, app ranking, channel inventory, hiring, or disclosed customer concentration;
- adversarial check: `risk`, `dilution`, `concentration`, `cancellation`, `write-down`, `regulatory`, `bear case`.

### 2. Run a broad, low-cost social scout

Use X and Reddit aggressively as parallel lead generators. When X is accessible, scan recent relevant posts from several authors. If the user has explicitly authorized their logged-in likes, reposts, bookmarks, or following feed, inspect that saved session read-only and prioritize recurring themes. Do not like, repost, follow, message, or publish.

Use a two-pass scout. In pass one, collect only lightweight metadata or search snippets: URL, author/community, date, visible engagement, one-line claim, query family, and likely proof target. Deduplicate near-identical claims before opening long threads. Score clusters on company specificity, novelty, source traceability, visual potential, recency, independent repetition, and presence of a serious counterargument. Engagement is a tie-breaker, not the main rank; high-view posts can be recycled consensus and low-view specialist posts can contain the edge. In pass two, deeply read only the top three to five promoted posts or threads and the source links they contain.

For promoted items, capture the exact claim, proposed mechanism, missing proof, disagreement in replies, and the primary document that could confirm or disprove it. X and Reddit wording, charts, and images are discovery material; do not copy them into the video without permission and rights review.

For an ordinary company-story request, use this default breadth target when both services are accessible:

- at least 20 relevant X posts from at least eight distinct authors;
- at least five X query families spanning company, product, counterparty, contract, supply chain, and bear-case terms;
- at least eight useful Reddit posts or substantive comment threads from at least three relevant communities;
- at least one bull, one bear, and one domain-specialist interpretation;
- deduplicate repeated claims and count both raw items examined and unique story clusters.

These are pass-one discovery targets, not full-reading or evidence quotas. Set a search time or token budget before starting, stop expanding a query family when it produces only duplicates, and reduce the target when the niche is sparse or access is blocked. Record the actual metadata count, deduplicated cluster count, promoted deep-read count, and limitation. Do not fill the quota with irrelevant posts.

Keep this wide discovery stage cheap. Use the fastest economical browsing-capable model available with `minimal` or `low` reasoning for search-query expansion, URL collection, metadata capture, short summaries, deduplication, candidate subject generation, and candidate-title generation. If an orchestrator is available, one economy scout may produce the candidate table while the main agent continues other work. The scout may label leads and uncertainty but may not approve claims, calculate valuation, or write the final verdict. Promote only the best three to six candidates to the stronger verification and synthesis stage; use higher reasoning only when cross-document comparison, contract interpretation, numerical reconciliation, or causal judgment needs it. Record the discovery model tier and reasoning effort in `story-scout.json`.

If X is blocked, logged out, rate-limited, or too sparse, record `X_SCAN=UNAVAILABLE|PARTIAL` and continue through the fallback ladder in [references/source-harvest.md](references/source-harvest.md). Reddit remains a normal discovery surface rather than only an emergency fallback. A failed X scan must not stop the production. Substack, Seeking Alpha, newsletters, and news headlines may add leads; SEC, issuer IR, counterparty filings, contracts, regulators, and other primary sources still control the finished claims.

### 3. Break the story into atomic claims

Split compound claims before verification. For example, these are separate claims:

- a cloud provider has a capacity backstop;
- the chip vendor owns equity in that provider;
- the vendor shares in revenue above a contractual floor;
- unused capacity runs the vendor's open models;
- the revenue-share percentage is 50%.

One supported premise does not validate the rest. Record each item in `claim-ledger.json` using the evidence grades in [references/evidence-ladder.md](references/evidence-ladder.md).

### 4. Select a story mode and finalists

Choose the candidates with the strongest combination of surprise, primary evidence, economic consequence, visual clarity, and current relevance. Use one of two modes:

- **Focused reveal:** one mechanism when it is strong enough to carry the full Short.
- **Connected dossier:** two to four distinct stories that prove one provocative identity thesis, such as `엔비디아는 GPU 회사가 아니라 AI 인프라 ETF다`. Each item must add a different mechanism, not repeat the same bullish point.

A connected dossier does not need to pretend the stories are one causal chain. Give each item a short reveal and economic meaning, then explain the common thesis. Never compress a connected dossier into a 45–60 second template. Budget the running time by item count: two stories target 75–100 seconds, three target 100–140 seconds, and four target 130–180 seconds or split into separate Shorts. Reserve at least 18–25 seconds of understandable screen time per story plus 10–15 seconds total for the shared hook and payoff. Choose the focused mode when the stories have no defensible common thesis or the platform/runtime constraint cannot preserve that floor. Prefer mechanisms over slogans:

```text
odd clue -> wrong mental model -> hidden contract or flow -> economic consequence -> strongest counterpoint -> visible closing payoff
```

In investment-story mode only, continue from that payoff into a dated valuation bridge, invalidation condition, and verdict.

Write the causal chain in three to six steps. Label the boundary between disclosed fact, calculation, inference, and market chatter. An unverified percentage, anonymous claim, or causal leap may be shown in a short `시장설` lane when it is materially interesting; never silently upgrade it into fact or let it carry the valuation verdict. The payoff should be the gap between the rumor and the documented structure.

### 5. Build the source-locked packet

Create these files before narration:

- `story-scout.json`: X, Reddit, fallback discovery log, search queries, actual item and author/community counts, model tier, reasoning effort, candidate clusters, and access limitations;
- `claim-ledger.json`: atomic claims, periods, units, evidence grade, URLs, and rejected extensions;
- `story-brief.md`: editorial mode, hook, answer, mechanism, economic consequence, counterpoint, closing payoff, and—only in investment-story mode—valuation bridge, verdict basis, and invalidation condition;
- `mechanism-map.json`: each visual object and arrow mapped to claim IDs, plus the persistent subject, three or more visible states, and the camera purpose for every mechanism sequence;
- `source-map.md`: dated primary links and a short description of what each one proves;
- `research-audit.md`: exact X and Reddit search counts, consulted URLs, candidates promoted or rejected, proof gaps, and a clear distinction between production-time research and any later audit.

Copy [assets/templates/story-scout-v2.json](assets/templates/story-scout-v2.json) for the discovery audit and [assets/templates/story-packet-v2.json](assets/templates/story-packet-v2.json) for the verified handoff when useful. Run:

```text
python scripts/validate_story_packet.py "<story-packet.json>"
```

Fix all errors before production. A passing validator does not replace reading the sources.

### 6. Write the Short around one reveal or one connected dossier

Let the locked narration determine the final shot lengths. A focused reveal follows one causal mechanism. A connected dossier opens with the umbrella identity claim, gives each of two to four stories enough time to show a distinct process, and closes by explaining the shared pattern. Do not force every story to share one diagram; preserve a recognizable company world and give each mechanism its own physical subject and visible state change.

For a **focused company story**, let the mechanism choose the duration. Use roughly 45–70 seconds for a compact single reveal. A connected dossier follows the item-count budget in Step 4 and normally runs longer. Prefer:

- **0–2 s:** show the impossible, dangerous, or counterintuitive state already happening;
- **2–8 s:** establish the familiar object and the viewer's wrong mental model;
- **8–18 s:** attempt the obvious explanation and visibly show why it is incomplete;
- **18–52 s:** enter, isolate, cut through, explode, or transform the subject while revealing the real mechanism;
- **52–68 s:** show the business consequence, counterforce, or risk inside the same world;
- **final 5–10 s:** deliver the answer, identity redefinition, or visual callback that resolves the first frame.

For a connected company dossier, use the middle section for two to four separate reveals and preserve at least 18–25 seconds per story. Return to the same logo, facility, product ecosystem, or map between reveals so the package feels like one world rather than unrelated cards. If the locked narration or storyboard falls below the per-story floor, remove an item or split the video instead of increasing speech speed.

For an **investment story**, retain the same animated hook and mechanism, then reserve only the time the conclusion actually needs for a compact ten-year valuation view, dated price, counterpoint, and explicit verdict. Do not place a P/E chart at the front merely because the production skill can generate one.

Treat these timelines as information budgets. The animated mechanism, economic consequence, and closing payoff receive narration priority in company-story mode. The mechanism, valuation basis, and verdict receive priority in investment-story mode. If the mechanism still cannot be understood, lengthen the Short within YouTube's eligible vertical format or split the topic instead of increasing speech density.

The source reveal should create tension: `X에서는 50%라고 돌지만, 계약서가 확인해 주는 건 여기까지입니다.` Never use this phrasing unless the stated boundary is accurate.

In focused mode, allow one compact **rumor lane**, normally five to eight seconds. In connected-dossier mode, up to three distinct C-grade leads may appear when each one adds a different mechanism and is paired immediately with the closest A-grade fact or the exact missing proof. Keep total rumor material to roughly one-third of the Short so the viewer still receives a documented payoff. Show the strongest version people are discussing, mark it visibly as `시장설`, `미확인`, `X발 주장`, `Reddit 주장`, or `연결 추정`, then pivot to `공시 확인`. A precise unverified number may be large on screen only when the uncertainty label is equally immediate and legible. Rumors may create the hook, supply texture, and shape the story package; documented facts and reproducible analysis must control the investment conclusion.

Translate specialist terms into conversational Korean before TTS. Speak the plain meaning, then keep the exact English term and legal boundary as a smaller on-screen label. For example, narrate `안 써도 최소 약정량은 돈을 내는 계약` and label it `Take-or-pay · 최소 사용량 지급 약정`. A metaphor may simplify the mechanism but may not erase scope, conditions, period, or uncertainty.

Do not illustrate the story as unrelated cards. Carry one persistent subject through a continuous sequence: establish it in the real world, isolate it, expose its hidden layer, animate the force or money flow, and show the economic consequence. A viewer should be able to follow the story with the sound muted.

### 6A. Package it with a provocative identity title

Write the YouTube title as an **identity claim, territorial expansion, hidden-control metaphor, or broken intuition**, not as a miniature earnings table. The title should make the viewer reconsider what the company actually is or controls. Prefer one short, conversational thought such as:

- `엔비디아는 ETF다`
- `엔비디아의 미친 영토 확장`
- `아마존은 쇼핑몰이 아니다`
- `보험회사가 AI 수혜주가 된 이유`

The strongest default is `company + provocative redefinition` or `company + vivid expansion/consequence`. Keep the exact numbers, P/E, growth rates, dates, and valuation range inside the video and description. Do not stack ticker, price, percentage, revenue, and P/E in the title. Use one number only when that single number is the mystery itself and removing it destroys the idea.

Aggressive wording is allowed, including `미친`, `삼켰다`, `장악했다`, `ETF다`, or `돈 복사 구조`, when the video clearly demonstrates the intended metaphor. Do not let the metaphor create a false legal or factual classification: `엔비디아는 ETF다` must be understood and explained as diversified AI-infrastructure exposure, not a claim that NVIDIA is legally an exchange-traded fund. The title may be hotter than the narration, but its underlying mechanism must resolve in the first half of the Short.

Generate at least three title candidates from different frames: `identity redefinition`, `territory/control`, and `hidden money flow`. Reject report-style titles that lead with two or more figures, a date, `실적 분석`, or a string of finance terms. The selected title and standalone thumbnail should complement each other rather than repeat the same sentence.

### 7. Build the story-motion master

Route every shot through [references/cinematic-story-production.md](references/cinematic-story-production.md). Match both the requested visual fidelity and the explanatory action. For a realistic reference-channel request, apply `cinematic-mechanism-shorts`: generated footage or a detailed tested 3D rig is the target, and a 2D/2.5D preview is not an equivalent finished result.

- Motion Canvas, SVG, or deterministic 2D animation is useful for abstract contracts, ownership, network and money flow when that visual treatment fits the brief;
- Remotion with CSS/SVG and limited Three.js is the next choice for controlled 2.5D company worlds, server racks, supply chains, product stacks, tracked callouts, and repeatable frame-accurate rendering;
- prevalidated reusable 3D rigs or pre-rendered subject-specific motion plates may be triggered when they fit the factual subject and camera need;
- new Blender scenes are reserved for mechanisms that truly need physical depth, cutaways, exploded views, construction, or camera travel and must pass a low-resolution blockout gate before full rendering;
- real licensed footage or photography for the establishing world and documentary proof;
- `moneyprinterturbo-video` for selected TTS, captions, audio, final encoding, thumbnail, and QA tasks after the animated picture plan is locked.

Do not ask MoneyPrinterTurbo's ordinary infographic templates to invent the company-story picture. Build a continuous motion master first, then use the parts of MoneyPrinterTurbo that improve delivery. In company-story mode, traditional stock cards and charts should occupy no more than roughly one-fifth of runtime unless the story itself is about a chart. Most narrated time should show the subject or environment changing, not merely the camera moving over a still image.

Do not attempt an unbounded new 3D scene directly at final quality. For every new Blender or nontrivial Three.js sequence, render a low-detail blockout, inspect the opening, midpoint, peak action, and exit frames, and test a short continuous motion proof. If repeated repair does not resolve collisions, clipping or spatial ambiguity, change the shot plan or use an explicitly lower-fidelity preview. Do not silently downgrade a cinematic request and report a style match. Prefer reusable parameterized rigs over unrelated B-roll.

For each focused story or dossier item, build an original subject-specific sequence with an establishing state, a genuine curiosity gap, an internal reveal, a causal action and a resulting state. Change the view at meaningful phrase/action boundaries. Many cuts can be short, but hold a complex visible mechanism long enough to understand it; do not impose a universal two-second cut. Camera motion alone may connect beats but does not count as the causal action.

Every arrow needs a named origin, destination, object, direction, and supporting claim ID. Every cutaway, x-ray, capacity fill, ownership link, or physical metaphor must remain within the claim ledger's truth level. Verify the motion itself at entry, peak action, consequence, and transition frames.

## Hard evidence boundaries

- A combined reporting segment cannot be presented as a pure sub-business. `Compute & Networking` is not the same as disclosed networking-only revenue.
- `Could`, `may`, and `if` remain conditional in Korean. A scenario is not a signed contract.
- Do not present an undisclosed percentage as fact. `Revenue share exists` does not prove `50% revenue share`; the 50% figure may appear only as visibly labeled market chatter with `비율 미공개` in the same beat.
- Two separately disclosed facts do not prove their connection. Cloud commitments used for open-model R&D plus an unused-capacity guarantee does not by itself prove that every idle GPU runs a named model; the possible connection may be discussed as `연결 추정` and followed by the missing evidence.
- Equity investment, purchase commitment, capacity reservation, customer prepayment, and operating expense are different cash-flow objects. Name the correct one.
- An indirect signal such as resale price, lead time, or app rank is a clue. Tie it to a primary operating metric and show alternative explanations before using it in the verdict.
- Do not imply inside information. The value comes from connecting public documents faster and more clearly.

## Completion gate

A research package is complete only when the focused story or every dossier item has an A-grade primary-source anchor, a visible fact-versus-inference boundary, a counterpoint to the overall thesis, claim-linked visuals, and a motion plan with a persistent company or infrastructure subject and explanatory state changes. Company-story mode additionally requires a closing payoff that resolves the opening question. Investment-story mode additionally requires a valuation bridge, dated price, verdict, and invalidation condition. The package must also include `research-audit.md` with actual X/Reddit breadth counts and limitations. If chatter is used, it must also have a source or `user_reported` origin, an uncertainty label, and a documented boundary. A video request is complete only after a verified motion master and final MP4, standalone thumbnail, source map, research audit, and upload package exist.
