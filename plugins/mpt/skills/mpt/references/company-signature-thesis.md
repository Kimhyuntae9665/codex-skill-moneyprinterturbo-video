# Company signature thesis for stock Shorts

Every company Short needs one memorable, company-specific explanation beyond valuation, dividend yield, or a list of quarterly figures. The viewer should be able to answer `이 회사는 사실 무엇을 지배하거나 반복해서 잘하는가?` after one viewing.

## Required output

Freeze these fields before scripting:

- `X_THESIS_SCAN=USED|UNAVAILABLE|CONFLICTED`
- `SIGNATURE_THESIS`: one conversational Korean sentence
- `WHY_ONLY_THIS_COMPANY`: the assets, relationships, behavior, or operating system that make the sentence company-specific
- `PRIMARY_CLAIM_IDS`: primary evidence for every factual premise
- `VISUAL_PROOF`: one real product, facility, network map, contract flow, or causal diagram
- `ECONOMIC_PAYOFF`: revenue, margin, cash flow, retention, pricing, share count, or valuation consequence
- `COUNTERPOINT`: one fact that could weaken the story

A Short without a frozen signature thesis is incomplete. The line must be spoken and visually demonstrated in the first half of the video, then paid off through a metric or the verdict.

## X discovery pass

When X is accessible, review at least three relevant posts from at least two independent authors. Prefer recent posts, but include an older durable thesis when the business mechanism is still current. Search with the ticker, company and product names plus terms such as:

- `moat`, `misunderstood`, `not just`, `full stack`, `ecosystem`;
- `distribution`, `network`, `pricing power`, `installed base`, `switching cost`;
- `supply`, `capacity`, `contract`, `backlog`, `cloud`, `neocloud`;
- `ARPU`, `ARPP`, `users`, `membership`, `float`, `royalty`, `franchise`, or another business-specific driver.

Record author, post date, URL, the proposed causal idea, and what primary evidence would be required. Use X to learn the vocabulary, debate, overlooked linkage, and visual metaphor. Do not use a post's popularity as proof, and do not copy its wording, chart, screenshot, or image without compatible rights.

If X is blocked, unavailable, thin, promotional, or contradictory, record the actual state. Continue from earnings calls, SEC filings, investor presentations, customer or supplier filings, and signed contract releases. Never write `X에서 화제` without evidence.

## Selection test

Score each candidate angle on five questions:

1. **Specific:** Would replacing the ticker with another large company make the sentence false?
2. **Causal:** Does it explain why a metric, moat, risk, or valuation outcome exists?
3. **Verifiable:** Can every factual premise be locked to an appropriate primary source?
4. **Visual:** Can the relationship be shown in one clear composition rather than a paragraph?
5. **Falsifiable:** Is there a metric or event that could weaken the thesis?

Reject generic labels such as `AI 수혜주`, `좋은 회사`, `강력한 브랜드`, `시장 지배자`, or `주주환원 기업` unless the sentence also names the mechanism and measurable consequence. Do not force a positive story; a company's signature may be a structural weakness.

## Script pattern

Use one of these placements:

- **Hook:** `[보이는 정체]로 알려졌지만, 돈은 사실 [숨은 시스템]에서 벌립니다.`
- **Answer:** pose the contradiction first, then reveal the signature system within 6-15 seconds.
- **Bridge:** connect the signature thesis to the hero metric: `[자산·관계 A]가 [행동 B]를 만들고, 그래서 [지표 C]가 움직였습니다.`

Give the signature beat one complete spoken sentence and one dedicated visual. Do not squeeze four independent moats into one crowded scene. A broad system may contain three linked layers only when the causal chain is clear.

## Examples of the required depth

These are structure examples, not reusable current claims.

### Meta

Weak: `메타는 세계 최대 SNS 회사입니다.`

Stronger structure: `메타는 Instagram·WhatsApp·Facebook에서 수십억 명의 관계와 관심 데이터를 모으고, 그 규모를 광고 단가와 사용자당 매출로 바꾸는 플랫폼입니다.`

- Use the issuer's deduplicated family metric for total people; never add overlapping app counts.
- Put the original date next to each product-level milestone.
- **WeChat is not a Meta product.** Use WhatsApp. If a user or source says WeChat, correct it before research, narration, captions, thumbnail, and charts.
- Pay off the story with Family DAP, engagement, ad impressions or price, ARPU/ARPP, revenue, and the relevant margin or investment risk.

### NVIDIA

Weak: `엔비디아는 GPU 회사가 아닙니다.`

Stronger structure: `엔비디아는 GPU만 파는 회사가 아니라 가속 컴퓨팅, 네트워킹, 시스템, CUDA 소프트웨어와 공급·클라우드 관계를 한 스택으로 묶어 AI 인프라의 선택지를 좌우합니다.`

- Separate chips, networking, complete systems, software ecosystem, manufacturing or supply commitments, and cloud-capacity agreements.
- Verify revenue and product claims in NVIDIA filings and presentations; verify a supplier, customer, neocloud, or contract relationship from both parties when material.
- Do not say NVIDIA owns or controls a neocloud merely because it invested in it, supplies GPUs, guarantees capacity, or signs a service agreement.
- Show the economic payoff through mix, networking growth, system content, recurring software or service revenue, supply visibility, pricing, or customer concentration. Pair it with export controls, customer internal chips, supply dependence, or capital intensity as the counterpoint.

### Other useful forms

- Franchise: store count is less distinctive than the royalty, rent, purchasing, and franchise-capital system that converts sales into parent cash flow.
- Insurer: premium growth is less distinctive than underwriting discipline plus investable float and the way capital returns interact with catastrophe or reserve risk.
- Membership retail: low merchandise margin can be the acquisition engine for recurring membership fees and renewal economics.
- Payments: transaction volume matters because acceptance, issuer, merchant, and fraud-data networks reinforce one another; distinguish network economics from lending risk.
- Industrial: the installed base, service attachment, certification, replacement cycle, and aftermarket margin may explain more than one quarter of equipment sales.

## Evidence and rights gate

- Treat X posts as `secondary_context` or discovery notes, never `primary_reported`.
- Lock financial, user, segment, contract, ownership, and supply-chain facts to SEC, issuer IR, official counterpart disclosures, or another appropriate primary source.
- A memorable sentence may compress verified facts but may not strengthen them. `supplier`, `investor`, `customer`, `partner`, `capacity buyer`, `owner`, and `controller` are different relationships.
- Use original visual construction and rights-cleared photos or logos. Do not reproduce an X creator's proprietary chart or annotated image merely because the post is public.
