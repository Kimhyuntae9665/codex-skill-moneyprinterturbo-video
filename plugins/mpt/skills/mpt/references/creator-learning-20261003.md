## Concrete production decisions

1. Add `format_family`, `topic_frame`, `viewer_continuity`, and one `next_question` to the brief. For this channel, a useful frame is “what a company event changes about earnings and the price investors pay.” Buyback execution → earnings per share → valuation can form a coherent sequence even across different business examples. Select related videos by the next actual question, not ticker overlap alone; changing Studio links still requires publication authorization.
2. For every shot, add `new_information` and `exit_reason` to the existing shot plan. A playing official source can satisfy its source-use quota while adding little information. Review separately whether a component, action, comparison, or consequence becomes newly visible. Replace weak source intervals rather than adding cosmetic zooms or switching headings.
3. Compare a reference and our output using a small table: opening question completion, semantic cuts, primary visual share, headline/caption repetition, voice delivery, and number comprehension. Record observed facts and unavailable playback/listening separately. Do not create an arbitrary viewing quota.
4. At phone size, remove a heading that repeats the caption or key metric. Prefer one short headline, one large proof visual, and the necessary spoken caption. Keep dates and financial qualifiers prominent when they change the meaning; do not simplify them away for visual cleanliness.
5. Test the first semantic beat and the densest financial beat before full rendering. Change one consequential choice where possible. Retain the existing dry-audio → silence edit → timing → pictures → mix order. Do not blindly apply 1.2–1.4 voice speed or a -60 dB threshold to every voice.

The existing 60% official-video minimum, 30% news/context minimum, claim lock, historical valuation contract, actual listening disclosure, and same-age channel observation loop remain in force. This note claims no measured retention or view-count improvement.
