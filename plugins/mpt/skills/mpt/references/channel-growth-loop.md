# YouTube Shorts growth loop

Use this reference when the requested output is intended for YouTube Shorts or when the user asks to improve reach, retention, subscribers, titles, thumbnails, or channel strategy.

## Strategy packet

Before drafting, assign a stable `strategy_id` and record:

- intended viewer and the question they already care about;
- `format_family`, `topic_frame`, and `viewer_continuity`: presentation format, shared viewer interest, and the reason the previous relevant episode's viewers would choose this one;
- `series` and the channel promise reinforced by the episode;
- `topic_hypothesis`: why the subject is timely or broadly understandable;
- three evidence-compatible hooks and the selected `hook_variant`;
- one complete payoff; investment-analysis additionally needs the price-aware verdict under `../SKILL.md`;
- `duration_profile`: `focused` (about 25-35 seconds), `full` (about 45-60 seconds), or a justified custom duration;
- two or three hero numbers and any supporting claims moved to the description;
- one production variable being tested. Keep the other major choices stable where practical.

Do not predict virality from a checklist. Topic interest, competition, distribution, and audience history can dominate production differences.

Expand a series through a neighboring interest rather than an unrelated popular topic. Record one natural `next_question` and suggested related episode, while answering the current opening question completely. Treat creator-reported view-count walls and fixed “test audience” sizes as unverified hypotheses; do not substitute them for the observation record below. See `creator-learning-20261003.md` for the evaluated user reference.

## Packaging

Create an `upload-package` beside the video with:

- three accurate title candidates and one selected title, spanning identity redefinition, territory/control, and hidden money flow;
- a title that leads with one provocative company-specific idea instead of stacked prices, percentages, P/E, dates, or ticker symbols; reserve routine numbers for the video and description;
- one standalone 9:16 thumbnail sentence that complements rather than repeats the selected title;
- the first two description lines stating the question and conclusion;
- the price/data as-of date, source links, material assumptions, and attribution required for assets;
- one specific, meaningful viewer question when discussion would add information.

Custom Shorts thumbnails may be available in YouTube Studio, so do not slow the spoken opening solely to expose a cover frame. Verify the current account UI at upload time. Native title/thumbnail A/B testing may not support Shorts; record `not_supported` instead of claiming a platform experiment. Do not duplicate-upload near-identical Shorts as the default test method.

## Observation record

When the user provides YouTube Studio exports or authorizes access to their channel analytics, record comparable snapshots at 24 hours, 72 hours, and 7 days after publication. Never invent a missing historical snapshot or treat a cumulative value as a same-age value.

Store when available:

- video ID, publication time and timezone, observation time, strategy ID, series, subject, hook variant, duration profile, selected title, and thumbnail sentence;
- views and engaged views as separate fields;
- stayed-to-watch rate, average view duration, average percentage viewed, subscribers gained, likes, comments, and traffic-source shares;
- audience-retention points or drop intervals and any relevant market/news event;
- missing or unavailable metrics as null, not zero.

Record the report surface/filter, exact reporting window and observation time separately from a video's publication time. Studio content-list totals, realtime estimates, selected-period analytics and same-age snapshots can differ; do not silently merge them. For stayed-to-watch, retain shown-in-feed/exposure counts when available and otherwise mark that denominator unknown. Small high-percentage samples cannot be ranked as winners against much larger samples merely by percentage.

When average percentage viewed exceeds 100%, record the value as displayed; repeat viewing/loops may contribute. It does not mean every person completed the video, and it does not replace the feed-entry rate. A high engaged-view average with a low stayed-to-watch rate calls for separate opening and after-entry hypotheses.

When channel access is provided for a skill review, inventory all visible uploads but distinguish that from the representative clips/frames actually inspected and from actual audio listening. Do not claim to have watched every upload from titles or thumbnails. Do not send private Studio analytics to external reviewers without authorization for that data and destination. Use current cumulative observations as current observations; do not reconstruct missing 24h/72h/7d history.

Link future observations to the exact skill/render version and production strategy ID. A channel made with an evolving skill does not establish that its current revision produced older episodes. Keep historical version unknown when no build record exists.

Average view duration and average percentage viewed among engaged viewers do not diagnose the initial swipe by themselves. Read stayed-to-watch and retention together. Keep Shorts-feed behavior separate from thumbnail impressions and click-through rate on surfaces where a thumbnail is shown.

## Decision rule

Compare similar topics and the same elapsed observation window. Prefer repeatable median movement across several Shorts over one outlier. Small samples, different news environments, and personalized distribution prevent strict causal claims.

- low distribution with reasonable engagement: revisit topic demand, timing, and audience fit before changing animation;
- distribution but low stayed-to-watch: change the first sentence and first visual;
- strong opening followed by a number-scene drop: reduce metric density and clarify period, unit, and consequence;
- completion without subscriber conversion: strengthen the recurring channel promise and publish a complete follow-up check;
- no comparable sample: record `INSUFFICIENT_DATA` and continue the controlled observation.

The review output must name one pattern to keep, one to stop or revise, and one next hypothesis. Do not create universal viral thresholds.
